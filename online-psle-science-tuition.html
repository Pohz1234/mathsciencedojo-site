Online PSLE Science Tuition in Singapore: Small Live Groups of Up to 5

For P5 and P6 students who understand the topics but lose marks in open-ended questions. Taught personally by Mr Yeo.

Short answer: MathScienceDojo runs live online PSLE Science classes for P5 and P6 in groups of up to 5, taught by Mr Yeo. A group session is $30 for 1 hour, one-to-one is $50, and you can try one lesson for $10 first. The focus is answering technique for Section B open-ended questions.

What your child gets

  • Keyword answering technique mapped to the marking scheme.
  • Open-ended question drills using real prelim papers.
  • Concept clinics for the topics that trip most students, such as energy, cycles and systems.
  • Exam-timing strategy, so the paper is finished with time to check.
  • A small live class where every answer is reviewed, every lesson.

Questions parents ask

How much does online PSLE Science tuition cost at MathScienceDojo? +

Primary 5 and 6 Science is $30 per 1-hour session in a group of up to 5 students, or $50 per session one-to-one. Billing is monthly, and you can try one lesson for $10 first.

How big are the classes? +

A group has at most 5 students, so Mr Yeo sees every student's working in each lesson.

Are the lessons live or recorded? +

All lessons are live online with Mr Yeo, not recorded videos. Class times are usually weekdays 3pm to 9pm and weekends 11am to 8pm, agreed with Mr Yeo.

Which levels does the PSLE Science class cover? +

Primary 5 and Primary 6 students preparing for PSLE Science, especially those who understand the concepts but lose marks in Section B open-ended questions.

You pay for one lesson, then agree a time with Mr Yeo on WhatsApp. He sees how your child answers and tells you where marks are being lost. If the class does not suit your child, he may recommend another teacher instead.
