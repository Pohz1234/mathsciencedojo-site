PSLE Maths Fractions: Fraction of a Quantity, Remainder and Units

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole. Most fraction problem sums are solved by turning the fractions into units, and most marks are lost by taking a fraction of the wrong whole.

Fraction of a quantity

  • Divide by the denominator, multiply by the numerator. To find 2/5 of 60, find 1/5 = 60 ÷ 5 = 12, then 2/5 = 2 × 12 = 24.
  • "Of" means multiply. 1/2 of 2/3 = 1/2 × 2/3 = 1/3.
  • Draw a bar split into the units of the denominator, and shade the parts you are told about.

Example 1: fraction of a quantity

Sam had $60. He spent 2/5 of it on a book. How much money did he have left?

1 unit = $60 ÷ 5 = $12
Spent: 2 units = 2 × $12 = $24
Left: 3 units = 3 × $12 = $36
Sam had $36 left.

Fraction of a remainder

Example 2: fraction of what is left

Ria spent 1/3 of her money on food and 1/2 of the remainder on a toy. She had $12 left. How much money did she have at first?

Total = 3 units. Food = 1 unit, remainder = 2 units.
Toy = 1/2 of 2 units = 1 unit. Left = 1 unit.
1 unit = $12
Total: 3 units = 3 × $12 = $36
Ria had $36 at first.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Taking 1/2 of the whole when the question says 1/2 of the remainder.
  • Adding or subtracting fractions without a common denominator.
  • Not writing what 1 unit stands for, so the method mark is lost.
  • Leaving a fraction answer unsimplified, or leaving out the unit.

Fractions questions

What is a fraction in PSLE Maths? +

A fraction shows a number of equal parts out of a whole, such as 2/5, which is 2 parts out of 5 equal parts.

How do you find a fraction of a quantity? +

Divide the quantity by the denominator to find 1 part, then multiply by the numerator. For 2/5 of 60, divide 60 by 5 to get 12, then multiply by 2 to get 24.

What is the difference between a fraction of the whole and a fraction of the remainder? +

A fraction of the whole is taken from the original amount. A fraction of the remainder is taken from what is left after something was already used, so it is a smaller amount.

How do I stop losing method marks in fraction problem sums? +

Draw a bar model, write what 1 unit stands for, show each step on its own line, and end with a sentence answer that has the unit.
