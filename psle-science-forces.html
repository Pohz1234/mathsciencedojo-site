PSLE Science Forces: Friction, Gravity and Elastic Spring Force Explained

A force is a push or a pull. Most marks in this topic are lost by naming a force without saying which way it acts, or by saying friction stops an object instead of slows it down.

Forces to name correctly

  • Friction: acts between surfaces in contact and acts in the opposite direction to the motion. A rougher surface produces more friction.
  • Gravitational force: pulls objects towards the Earth.
  • Elastic spring force: a stretched or compressed spring pulls or pushes back to its original shape.
  • Magnetic force: a push or pull between magnets, or between a magnet and a magnetic material.
  • A force can change the speed, the direction or the shape of an object.

How to write the full-mark answer

Use this order: (1) name the force, (2) say which way it acts, (3) say what it does to the motion of the object in the question.

Example: toy car on carpet

A toy car rolls down a ramp onto tiles and then onto a carpet. Explain why it stops sooner on the carpet.

The carpet is rougher than the tiles, so there is more friction between the car and the carpet. Friction acts in the opposite direction to the motion, so the car slows down and stops sooner.

Example: ball thrown up

Explain why a ball thrown upwards comes back down.

Gravitational force pulls the ball towards the Earth. It slows the ball down as it rises, and then pulls it back down.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Writing that gravity "pushes". Gravitational force pulls towards the Earth.
  • Writing that friction "stops" a moving object. It slows it down.
  • Not saying that friction acts in the opposite direction to the motion.
  • Writing "force" without naming the type of force.

Forces questions

What is a force in PSLE Science? +

A force is a push or a pull. It can change the speed, direction or shape of an object.

What is friction and how does it affect motion? +

Friction is a force between surfaces in contact. It acts in the opposite direction to the motion and slows a moving object down. A rougher surface produces more friction.

What is gravitational force? +

Gravitational force is the pull of the Earth on objects, which pulls them towards the Earth.

What is elastic spring force? +

Elastic spring force is the force a stretched or compressed spring exerts to return to its original shape.
