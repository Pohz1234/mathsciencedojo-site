PSLE Maths · P5–P6 · Ratio

PSLE Maths Ratio: draw the units, find 1 unit, then answer.

A ratio compares quantities as equal parts called units. Most ratio problem sums are solved by finding the value of 1 unit, and most marks are lost by skipping that step or by missing which quantity stays the same.

The units method in four steps

  • 1. Draw a bar for each quantity, with the number of units the ratio gives.
  • 2. Find the total units by adding the parts of the ratio.
  • 3. Find 1 unit. Divide the amount you are given by the units it covers, and write what 1 unit stands for.
  • 4. Answer the question asked, with a sentence and the unit.

Example 1: the total is given

Ali and Ben have stickers in the ratio 3 : 5. They have 120 stickers altogether. How many stickers does Ben have?

Ali 3 units, Ben 5 units, total 3 + 5 = 8 units.
8 units = 120 stickers
1 unit = 120 ÷ 8 = 15 stickers
Ben: 5 units = 5 × 15 = 75 stickers
Ben has 75 stickers.

When a quantity changes, find what stays the same

  • The total stays the same. This happens when one person gives to another. The total number of units is equal before and after.
  • The difference stays the same. This happens when both quantities increase or decrease by the same amount. The difference in units is equal before and after.
  • One quantity stays the same. Make the units of that quantity equal in both ratios, then compare.

Example 2: the total stays the same

Mei and Lin have beads in the ratio 5 : 3. Mei gives Lin 20 beads, and then they have the same number. How many beads did Mei have at first?

Before: Mei 5 units, Lin 3 units, total 8 units.
After: they are equal, so each has 8 ÷ 2 = 4 units.
Mei went from 5 units to 4 units, so 1 unit = 20 beads.
Mei at first: 5 × 20 = 100 beads.
Mei had 100 beads at first.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Not writing what 1 unit stands for. The method mark for that line is lost even when the answer is right.
  • Using the wrong quantity as the total, for example dividing by 5 instead of 3 + 5 = 8.
  • Not checking which quantity stays the same before and after a change.
  • Ending without a sentence answer or the unit, such as writing 75 instead of 75 stickers.

Ratio questions

What is a ratio in PSLE Maths? +

A ratio compares two or more quantities of the same kind, such as 3 : 5. It tells you how many equal parts, or units, each quantity has.

How do you solve a ratio problem sum using units? +

Draw one bar of equal units for each quantity. Add the units to find the total number of units, divide the given amount by that total to find the value of 1 unit, then multiply to find each quantity.

What do you do when a quantity changes in a ratio question? +

First find which quantity stays the same. If the total stays the same, as when one person gives items to another, keep the total units equal before and after. If the difference stays the same, keep the difference in units equal before and after.

How do you avoid losing method marks in ratio questions? +

Write what 1 unit stands for, show the working line by line, and finish with a sentence answer that has the unit, such as 75 stickers.
