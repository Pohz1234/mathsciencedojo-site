The real problem

Most P5–P6 students don't have a knowledge problem.
They have a marks problem.

They revise, they understand the concept, they can explain it to you at home — and still come back with 6 marks gone. Here's where those marks actually leak.

LEAK 01

Missing the marking keywords

PSLE Science open-ended answers are marked against specific keywords. A correct idea written in the "wrong" words scores zero. We train students to answer in the language the marking scheme rewards.

LEAK 02

Method marks thrown away

In Maths, students rush working, skip statements, or present answers without units. We build the habits that protect every method mark — even when the final answer slips.

LEAK 03

Question misreading under pressure

Under exam timing, students answer the question they expected, not the one asked. We drill question analysis until identifying what's really being tested becomes automatic.

How lessons work

A method built around how PSLE papers are actually marked.

1

Diagnose where marks are leaking

Mr Yeo reviews your child's recent papers before anything else. Every student's leak pattern is different — teaching starts from the actual evidence, not a generic syllabus march.

2

Teach the concept, then the answering technique

Understanding is only half the mark. Students learn the keyword structures examiners look for in Science and the working presentation that secures method marks in Maths.

3

Drill with real exam questions, live

Small classes mean Mr Yeo watches each student's working in real time and corrects mistakes in the same lesson — before they harden into habits.

4

Track the score, not just the effort

Parents see progress in percentages, not vague reassurance. If a topic isn't converting into marks yet, it stays on the plan until it does.
