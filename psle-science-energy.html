PSLE Science Energy: Forms of Energy and Energy Conversion Explained

Energy cannot be created or destroyed. It changes from one form to another. Most marks are lost by saying energy is used up, or by naming the wrong type of potential energy.

Forms of energy to name correctly

  • Kinetic energy: energy of a moving object.
  • Gravitational potential energy: stored energy of an object because of its height.
  • Elastic potential energy: stored energy in a stretched or compressed object such as a spring.
  • Chemical potential energy: stored in food, fuel and batteries.
  • Light, heat, sound and electrical energy. The Sun is the main source of energy on Earth.

How to write the full-mark answer

Use this order: (1) name the starting form of energy, (2) say it is converted to the next form, (3) name any other forms it also changes into.

Example: a ball is dropped

Explain the energy changes when a ball is dropped from a height and hits the ground.

The ball has gravitational potential energy at the top. As it falls, the gravitational potential energy is converted to kinetic energy. When it hits the ground, the kinetic energy is converted to sound energy and heat energy.

Example: a torch

State the energy conversion when a torch is switched on.

The chemical potential energy in the batteries is converted to electrical energy, which is then converted to light energy and heat energy.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Writing "energy is used up" or "energy is lost". Write that energy is converted to another form.
  • Writing "potential energy" without saying gravitational, elastic or chemical.
  • Forgetting that a falling or moving object also gives out sound and heat energy.
  • Mixing up heat and temperature.

Energy questions

What are the forms of energy in PSLE Science? +

The main forms are kinetic, potential (gravitational, elastic and chemical), light, heat, sound and electrical energy.

What is energy conversion? +

Energy conversion is energy changing from one form to another, such as chemical potential energy in a battery changing to electrical energy and then to light energy.

What is the difference between kinetic and potential energy? +

Kinetic energy is the energy of a moving object. Potential energy is stored energy, such as the energy an object has because of its height or because it is stretched.

Why is the Sun important as an energy source? +

The Sun is the main source of energy on Earth. Plants use light energy from the Sun to make food, and animals get energy from that food.
