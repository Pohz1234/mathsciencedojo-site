PSLE Science · P5–P6 · Plants

PSLE Science Plants: Photosynthesis and the Transport System Explained

Plants make their own food. Most marks in this topic are lost by saying plants take in food from the soil, or by mixing up what xylem and phloem carry.

What each part does

  • Leaves make food by photosynthesis, using light, carbon dioxide and water, and give out oxygen.
  • Roots absorb water and mineral salts from the soil.
  • Xylem carries water and mineral salts from the roots up to the leaves.
  • Phloem carries food made in the leaves to other parts of the plant.

How to write the full-mark answer

Use this order: (1) name the factor in the question, (2) link it to photosynthesis or transport, (3) say what happens to the plant.

Example: plant in the dark

Plant A is placed near a window and Plant B is placed in a dark cupboard. Both are given the same amount of water. Explain why Plant B grew poorly.

Plant B did not receive light, so it could not carry out photosynthesis. It made less food, so it did not have enough food for growth.

Example: coloured water

A white flower stem is placed in red-coloured water. After some hours the petals turn red. Explain why.

The coloured water was absorbed and carried up the stem through the xylem to the petals.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Writing that plants take in food from the soil. Plants make their own food in the leaves.
  • Swapping the roles: xylem carries water, phloem carries food.
  • Writing "sunlight" only, instead of linking light to photosynthesis.
  • Forgetting to say what happens to the plant, such as less food made so poor growth.

Plants questions

What is photosynthesis in PSLE Science? +

Photosynthesis is how plants make food in their leaves, using light, carbon dioxide and water. Oxygen is given out.

What is the difference between xylem and phloem? +

Xylem carries water and mineral salts from the roots up to the leaves. Phloem carries food made in the leaves to other parts of the plant.

Why does a plant in the dark grow poorly? +

Without light, the plant cannot carry out photosynthesis, so it makes less food and does not have enough for growth.

Do plants take in food from the soil? +

No. Plants make their own food in the leaves by photosynthesis. The roots absorb water and mineral salts from the soil.
