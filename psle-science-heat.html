PSLE Science · P5–P6 · Heat

PSLE Science Heat: what it is, how it moves, and the words that earn the marks.

Heat is energy that flows from a hotter object to a colder one. Most marks in this topic are lost by mixing up heat and temperature, or by explaining without the marking keywords.

Heat vs temperature

Temperature tells you how hot or cold something is. It is measured with a thermometer in degrees Celsius. Heat is the energy that moves from a hotter object to a colder object. An object gains heat when it gets hotter and loses heat when it gets colder.

Three ways heat travels

  • Conduction. Heat travels through a solid (or between solids touching). Metals are good conductors of heat. Wood, plastic, rubber, cloth and air are poor conductors (insulators).
  • Convection. Heat travels through liquids and gases. Warm liquid or gas expands, becomes less dense and rises. Cooler, denser liquid or gas sinks to take its place.
  • Radiation. Heat travels as rays and needs no material, so it can pass through space. This is how the Sun heats the Earth.

How to write the full-mark answer

Use this order: (1) name the property, (2) say which way the heat flows, (3) link it to what the person or object feels or does.

Example: why does a metal spoon feel colder than a wooden spoon?

Metal is a good conductor of heat. It conducts heat away from the hand faster than wood, which is a poor conductor. The hand loses heat faster to the metal spoon, so the metal spoon feels colder.

Mistakes that cost marks

  • Writing "the metal is colder". Both spoons are at the same room temperature. The difference is how fast the hand loses heat.
  • Writing "cold flows into the hand". Only heat flows, from hot to cold.
  • Leaving out "good conductor" or "poor conductor" in the answer.

Heat questions

What is heat in PSLE Science? +

Heat is a form of energy that flows from a hotter object to a colder object. An object gains heat when it gets hotter and loses heat when it gets colder.

What is the difference between heat and temperature? +

Temperature tells you how hot or cold something is and is measured in degrees Celsius with a thermometer. Heat is the energy that moves between objects. A thermometer measures temperature, not heat.

How do you answer a PSLE Science question on why a metal spoon feels colder than a wooden spoon? +

State that metal is a good conductor of heat, so it conducts heat away from the hand faster than wood, a poor conductor. Because the hand loses heat quickly to the metal, it feels colder.

What are the three ways heat travels? +

Conduction in solids, convection in liquids and gases, and radiation through space without needing any material.
