Pricing questions

How much does tuition cost? +

Per 1-hour online session: Primary 3–4 is $40 (1-1 only). Primary 5–6 is $50 for 1-1 or $30 in a group. Secondary 1–2 is $60 for 1-1 or $40 in a group. Billing is monthly.

What does the $10 trial include? +

A trial lesson with Mr Yeo. Pay by card or PayNow, then you land on WhatsApp to agree a time. If the trial doesn't suit your child, Mr Yeo may recommend another teacher instead of pushing you to continue.

Group or 1-1: which is better? +

1-1 gives the most personal attention and a plan built around one child's mistakes. Group (max 5) costs less and works well when a child learns from hearing other students' questions. Twins can join a group together on separate computers, each paying the group rate.

When are lessons held? +

Usually weekdays 3pm–9pm and weekends 11am–8pm. The exact timing is agreed directly with Mr Yeo.

What if my child misses a lesson? +

Make-up lessons are allowed if you let Mr Yeo know at least 24 hours before the class. Otherwise the lesson counts as taken.

How does billing work? +

Billing is monthly. Mr Yeo sends the invoice in the first week of each month.
